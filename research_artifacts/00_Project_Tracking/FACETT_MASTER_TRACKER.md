## 1. Status Classification Rubric

To ensure strict academic integrity and audit transparency, all requirements are categorized under four mutually exclusive statuses:
- **`COMPLETED`**: Fully implemented in the AgeWeaver codebase, validated by passing unit/integration tests, and supported by empirical experiment records in `outputs/experiments/`.
- **`PARTIAL`**: Partially implemented or adapted. The foundational logic exists (e.g. prompt rules, metric computation, parameter scaling), but full execution across all paper benchmarks (or GPU scale) is in progress or qualitatively validated.
- **`BLOCKED`**: Implementation or validation cannot proceed without external assets or dependencies (e.g. missing longitudinal target-age reference pairs for celebrities, or lack of direct GPU compute locally).
- **`NOT RUN`**: Architectural features specific to FaceTT's proprietary U-Net modification (e.g. internal cross-attention layer hooks or spherical Jacobian manifolds) that were not included in AgeWeaver's lightweight diffusion img2img scope.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Data Isolation Rule:** All empirical values reported by the original authors in the FaceTT paper are explicitly labeled as **[FaceTT Reported]**. All empirical values generated by our framework are explicitly labeled as **[AgeWeaver Measured]**. Under no circumstances are external numbers conflated with local results.
